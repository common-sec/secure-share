Secure Share

Send a password or a file privately. It goes straight to the other person and works only once.

Send passwords and files securely, browser to browser

Type a password, a note or an API key, or add files, and get a one-time link. Send the link in your usual chat or email and tell the other person a few words, out loud or another way. Their browser connects straight to yours and receives it, encrypted end to end.

  • Nothing is uploaded or stored. There is no server that keeps your data: it travels directly between the two browsers.
  • The link alone isn’t enough. The words are needed too, and they never go in the link.
  • It works once. After delivery, or after a time limit, the link is dead.
  • No account, no app to install. Any recent browser on a computer or phone.
