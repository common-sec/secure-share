Strong passwords and memorable passphrases

Generate a passphrase you can actually remember, like Stone~Behind~Karrotish~Pencils42~Over~Quail, or a password of random characters. The page shows how strong each one is, in bits of entropy, computed exactly from how it was generated.

  • Not just dictionary words: words are bent, mixed with capitals, numbers and separators at random, so word-list attacks don’t fit.
  • Generated in your browser with its cryptographic random number generator. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Shareable settings: a settings link holds your choices, never a password.
